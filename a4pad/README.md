## 1. Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

### 1.1. Overview
A4Pad ("the App") is designed to protect your privacy. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use the App. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data, notes, images, or documents.

### 1.2. Data Collection and Usage

**We Do Not Collect Personal Data**
We do not collect names, email addresses, Apple IDs, device identifiers (IDFA), IP addresses, or location data. We do not access, view, or analyze usage of your notes, sketches, or imported documents.

**Apple App Analytics**
The App uses Apple’s standard App Analytics service (App Store Connect), which may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data if you have opted-in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings. This data is **not linked to your identity** and is used solely to improve app performance and reliability.
- **Data Type:** Usage Data, Diagnostics
- **Purpose:** App Functionality, Analytics
- **Tracking:** No. We do not track you across other apps or websites for advertising purposes. Apple Analytics data is aggregated and does not constitute "Tracking" under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

### 1.3. Data Storage
**Local Storage Only**
All content you create, including notebooks, pages, drawings, and imported images, is stored **locally on your device**.
- We do not use Apple's CloudKit or iCloud Drive for syncing.
- We do not operate any external servers.
- **You obtain a copy of your data by exporting it as PDF.**

### 1.4. Permissions
The App requests specific permissions solely to provide functionality:
- **Photo Library:** Accessed only when you explicitly choose to import an image. We do not scan your library.
- **Camera:** Accessed only when you capture a new photo/scan for a page.
- **Files:** Accessed only when you import a document.

### 1.5. GDPR Compliance (EU)
A4Pad is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not collect or process any personal data. Apple may process anonymous usage and diagnostic data on our behalf through App Analytics. Your content remains strictly yours and under your control.

### 1.6. No Third-Party Tracking
We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel), advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not share data with data brokers or third parties.
