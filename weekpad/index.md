## 2. Local by Default

By default, all your data is stored ONLY on this device. We do not transmit

⚠️ We are not liable for any data loss, for any reason.

## 3. Optional iCloud Sync

Sync is optional, off by default, and included — but unsupported and provided as-is. If you
turn it on, your planners and photos are stored in YOUR own Apple iCloud
account (a private database only you and Apple can access). They are not files
or folders you can browse — only WeekPad can open them, and you will only see
WeekPad as a storage amount in your iCloud settings.

We still have no server and no database. We cannot read, see, reach, control,
or recover your synced data. Apple's iCloud terms apply to that copy. Syncing
images uses network data and counts toward your iCloud storage; if your iCloud
is signed out or full, syncing simply pauses and nothing local is lost.

## 4. Sync Is Not a Backup

Sync keeps your devices matching each other — it is NOT a backup. If you
delete or damage an entry, that change copies to your other devices. WeekPad
has NO import and NO restore: we cannot load data back into the app for you.
Your own Export is the only recovery that never depends on us, on Apple, or
on Sync.

⚠️ Sync is provided as-is, at your own risk. It keeps your devices matching each
other — it isn't a backup, and a change or deletion travels to your other
devices. Because your planners live in your own iCloud, we can't see them or put
them back for you. Export regularly, and keep anything important stored safely
elsewhere too.

## 5. Actions Outside WeekPad

Once Sync is on, your data lives in your iCloud, which you control from places
we cannot see — the iOS Settings app, iCloud storage management, another
device, or icloud.com. Signing out of iCloud, turning iCloud off for WeekPad,
deleting WeekPad's data there, or running out of space can change how the app
behaves or remove your synced planners. We cannot detect, prevent, undo, or
recover from these actions. Keeping your iCloud account and its settings in
working order is your responsibility.

## 6. No Tracking

WeekPad contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no data
brokers. We do not track your location or usage patterns.

## 7. Your Rights

Since we do not store your data, we cannot delete it for you or provide a
copy. You can export your data at any time using the Export feature, or delete
it by deleting planners, deleting the app, or (for synced data) removing
WeekPad's data from your iCloud settings.

## 8. Contact & Support

If you have questions about this policy, please assume the answer is
"We don't know, we don't have your data."
